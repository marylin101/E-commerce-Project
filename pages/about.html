About The Study Desk

A small team, a simple goal- make finding good stationery effortless.

Students, stationery enthusiasts and other stationery users all need convenient access to quality stationary products like pens, notebooks, and other academic supplies. Traditional ways include having to physically visit stores and search through aisles of products and rely on available of some items in particular areas. This is time consuming and can make the whole process less convenient for them.

The Study Desk is an e-commerce platform developed by three IT collage students designed to to address this issue. Our platform allows the customers to browser the multiple available stationery products, view a specific products detail and add it to shopping cart all in one go at your own convinience.

A lot of thought was put into this platform's design aiming to better user experience. Our frontend developer reviews a dozen different websites in order to provide platform that even a first-time online shopper can navigate through, with our simple and uncluttered design.
